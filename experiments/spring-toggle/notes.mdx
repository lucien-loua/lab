## Notes

Three spring presets on the same toggle. Same component, same payload, different feel.

- `gentle` reads as careful and slow. Good for settings panels that should not feel reactive.
- `snappy` is the production default. Settles in one frame, no overshoot, no perceived lag.
- `bouncy` calls attention to itself. Reserve it for celebratory affordances (success, completion).

Final pick: `snappy`. Toggles are committal actions and the user wants confirmation, not commentary.
